Targets are yours. Fuel never moves them and never scolds. Over is a number.

Net carbs

Carbs minus fiber. When this is on, tap the Carbs bar on Today to flip it to net carbs and back. An entry with no fiber number counts all its carbs, and the bar says how many there are. Fuel does not subtract sugar alcohols.

Nutrients

A nutrient that is on gets a row on Today and a tile on every sheet, up to two at once. A target is yours to set; until then the row is a plain number. DV is the label’s Daily Value, a bearing and never counted; for cholesterol and added sugars the label means it as a ceiling. An entry with no number for it does not count, and the row says “at least” and how many. A label read keeps every line it printed, so a switch turned on later reads them back.

Snap needs the family’s own code once, on each phone. It lets this phone send label photos to the family’s reader; without it nothing is sent. The code lives on this phone only, never in the app’s file.

What Fuel keeps, and where. Your entries, your pantry and your targets live on this phone only. Snap a label sends the label photo to the family’s own reader, which passes it to Anthropic to be read and keeps nothing; that is the only thing Fuel ever sends, and only when you tap Snap. A saved copy is a plain text file of the same; a shared card is a picture of one day, handed to the share sheet the same way. Fuel asks the phone to keep them even when it runs low on space; Storage, above, shows what the phone answered.

How the read works. The sentence on the tally is arithmetic on what you logged, your targets and the clock. Fuel never guesses what you ate.

The clock is the meal. Every entry keeps the time it was eaten. There are no meal names.
